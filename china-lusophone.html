China & Portuguese-speaking Countries

Tourism, health-system and travel-health indicators for China, Macao SAR and the nine member states of the Community of Portuguese Language Countries (CPLP). Macao has hosted the Forum for Economic and Trade Co-operation between China and Portuguese-speaking Countries since 2003.

World Bank data for Macao SAR covers tourism and population but few health-system indicators. Tourism series for some countries end several years ago. Each value shows its own year.

Travel health risks

Disease burden and immunization coverage that matter for travellers moving between these countries. Higher malaria, tuberculosis and HIV values and lower measles coverage mean higher risk.

Visitors to Macao from Portuguese-speaking countries

Annual visitor arrivals classified by place of issue of travel document, from Macao DSEC. This is not the same as place of residence. DSEC does not list the other seven CPLP countries separately.

2017 and 2018 are published only in thousands (Portugal 16.3k and 15.6k, Brazil 11.0k and 11.6k; DSEC Yearbook of Statistics 2019, Table 9.1), so they are left blank in the table. 2020–2022 reflect COVID-19 entry restrictions. DSEC changed its classification around 2015, so 2015 may not be fully comparable with later years.
