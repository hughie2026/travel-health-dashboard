Data & Methods

Every dataset on this site is publicly available without registration or an API key. A GitHub Actions workflow downloads the automated sources every week and stores them as static JSON files.

Macao visitor figures

Figures for Portugal and Brazil come from these DSEC publications:

  • 2015–2016: Macao Moderate Economic Diversification Statistical Indicator System 2016, Table 10, p. 18.
  • 2019–2022: Macao Moderate Economic Diversification Statistical Indicator System 2022, Table 27, p. 42.
  • 2017–2018 (thousands only, not shown in the table): Yearbook of Statistics 2019, Table 9.1, p. 162.

DSEC classifies these arrivals by place of issue of travel document, which differs from place of residence. Angola, Cabo Verde, Equatorial Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Mozambique, São Tomé and Príncipe and Timor-Leste are not listed separately in the published tables.

Methods

China & Portuguese-speaking countries. China, Macao SAR and the nine CPLP member states: Angola, Brazil, Cabo Verde, Equatorial Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Mozambique, Portugal, São Tomé and Príncipe, and Timor-Leste.

Scatter chart. For each country, the chart uses the most recent year within the last 10 years in which both selected indicators have a value.

Hospital beds per 1,000 arrivals. (beds per 1,000 residents × population ÷ 1,000) ÷ international arrivals × 1,000. Each input uses its own latest year, shown in the table.

Limitations

World Bank data for Macao SAR lacks most health-system indicators. Macao Health Bureau statistics would be needed to fill this gap.

Some World Bank tourism series update slowly, and the latest year differs by country.

Smaller countries such as Guinea-Bissau and São Tomé and Príncipe have many missing years.

Macao visitor figures for 2020–2022 reflect COVID-19 entry restrictions. DSEC changed its classification around 2015, so 2015 may not be fully comparable with later years.

OpenStreetMap coverage varies by city. City areas are approximate bounding boxes; the Macao box also covers parts of Hengqin and Zhuhai.

Differences between countries describe countries, not individuals, and do not show cause and effect.
